Calculation conventionsChinese dates and actual month boundaries use the browser’s Chinese calendar (ICU), with the existing gallery’s civil-date convention. Both clocks use the same selected local civil date and same modern animal pictograms for comparison. Chinese lunar ring shows astronomical elongation; Tibetan lunar ring shows numbered lunar dates. Phugpa arithmetic, following Svante Janson's Tibetan Calendar Mathematics (E806 epoch). Date boundaries use visitor-local civil dates in this prototype. Lunar pips indicate the actual civil-day starts of each numbered lunar date; omitted dates share a boundary, doubled dates span two days. The Moon icon is an independent approximate astronomical phase. Original modern pictograms, not historical Tibetan iconography. Reference calculator.
